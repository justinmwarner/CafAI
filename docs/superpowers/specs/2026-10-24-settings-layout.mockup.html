Settings: four ways to lay it out

Every option uses the same seven groups and the same controls as today, regrouped; the new pieces (status under each group, the coach's usage and cost, search) are marked NEW. Drawn at the Decent tablet's 1280×800, dark, Crema, with the coach on an API key.

A. Two panes: groups on the left, the chosen one on the right Recommended

As on an iPad or Android tablet's own Settings. Everything is one tap away, each group says how it stands without opening it, and the right side of the tablet is no longer empty. On a phone the list is a page of its own and a tap opens the group, with a back arrow, as History opens a bag. Each group has its own address (#/settings/coach), so "Set it in Settings" links, and a drink's Go back, land on the right page. The list is Settings' own first column, not an app-wide sidebar (PRODUCT.md rules those out).
