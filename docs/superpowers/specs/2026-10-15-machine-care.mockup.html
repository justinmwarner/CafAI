Machine care: the cleaning guide (D151, the first item)

Settings gets a Machine care section; its Cleaning guide opens a screen of its own, #/care, under Settings (the Settings place stays lit, and a back arrow returns there). The four places in the top bar stay as they are: at 962 px the bar has 18 px to spare, no room for a fifth. Later the stats card's "Weekly clean due" line (the third item) opens the same screen on the right routine.

The screen splits Decent's routine by how often: Daily · Weekly · Monthly · As needed, one at a time, so a routine fits on the tablet without scrolling. Each step is what to do, when, and a line on how, with its moment in Decent's video ("How often/to clean DE1", Decent Espresso Tech, 2022, 22 min). Where the video and Decent's current docs differ, the step follows the docs and says so in a quiet note. The list is CafAI's own text: it works offline.

The video loads only when you tap play or a moment, from youtube-nocookie.com. It plays in place in a browser and inside Decaid on Android (Decent's tablets). Inside Decaid on Windows, a Mac or an iPad it cannot: Decaid sends any page a frame loads to the system browser (tried on Decaid 0.8.8 for Windows), so there each moment is a link that opens YouTube at that moment instead.
