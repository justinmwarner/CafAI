Recommended layout. Figtree throughout, sentence-case labels, larger tabular readouts, tonal surfaces instead of outlines everywhere. Every color comes from one tint plus the mode; the graph colors stay semantic in every tint.

Shot startsThe main pane (tablet) or the Machine tab (phone) shows the big live graph. Not while you are typing to the coach: then only the live strip appears.
Shot is doneWhen the chat gets the shot's card (or its notice), the screen goes back to the coach. If you moved yourself during the shot, it stays where you are.
Back to the chartTablet: the preview chart in the machine column, or Shot in the top bar. Phone: the live strip on the coach, the Machine tab, or the sparkline on the shot card.
