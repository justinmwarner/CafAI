Steam, hot water and flush, live

When the machine starts steaming, pouring hot water or flushing, from CafAI's top bar, the group head or Decaid, the screen under the top bar turns into that drink's own view, as it does for a shot. Left: the dial, filling toward the drink's own target (the steam time, the hot water volume, the flush time) with a small scene that moves only while the machine runs. Right: what the machine reports, then the run so far. Under it: the drink's settings, the very steppers of Settings > Steam, hot water and flush.

Stop stays where it is, in the top bar, in the place of the start that runs. Every setting is held while anything runs (the machine is using them) and comes alive the moment it stops. Once it's done the view stays, with the result and Go back, which returns to the screen the view took over. Every loop here (the wisps, the stream, the wave, the live dot) holds still under reduced motion; the arc, the level and the numbers still move, because they are the readings.

TABLET · STEAM, RUNNING
The dial counts the steam time toward the time set (50 s here), in tenths like the shot timer; the arc's tip is where the machine is. Inside, the milk's surface turns and wisps rise, only while it steams. Ink, not a colour: steam is white.
  • Steam is the heater's temperature beside the one set; Pressure and Flow are the steam's, in Decent's colours, and the trace under them is the steam so far: a wand that sputters shows here.
  • The header names the phase in words: "Heating the steam…", "Steaming", "Purging the wand", "Done".
  • Steam settings are the same steppers as Settings, held while the machine runs (it is using them), with the reason on the same line. Stop is where Steam was.

TABLET · HOT WATER, RUNNING
The dial fills toward the volume set: the water's level rises inside it as the millilitres come, with the stream falling into it (behind the number, never across it). Blue, the flow's colour, for both water drinks.
  • Flow beside what is set; Scale when one is connected (Decaid tares it as the water starts and stops at the volume as grams); Time with the limit that stops it if the volume does not. No live temperature: during hot water the machine's mix temperature is not the water's, so CafAI leaves it out.
  • Hot water's four settings are held until it stops (Decaid fixes its weight stop as the water starts); one row here, one per row on a phone.

TABLET · FLUSH, DONE
Done: the view stays, with the result and Go back. Go back returns to the screen the view took over (the coach here; Settings or History if that is where the barista was), or the coach when there is none.
  • The dial keeps the final time with a check; the readouts become the run's: water through the group, the average flow, the group's temperature at the end.
  • The settings come alive the moment it stops, so a flush that was too short is one tap from a longer one; the next Flush in the top bar uses it.
