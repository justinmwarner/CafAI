Drink scenes: a choice of animations for steam, hot water and flush, and going back by itself

Asked: "make the steaming animation look better, maybe with the colours changing based on the numbers we get back; maybe latte art, or more of a nozzle and spray", and for pour, rinse and steam "a setting to go back by itself after some time, which can be turned off and on, with its time". Answered on the first draft of this page: every scene is an option, picked per drink in Settings, and hot water and flush get the same treatment; going back by itself is per drink, on, after 10 s, out of the box.

One made-up run per drink plays in every dial at once: Heating, the drink at work (a 50 s steam, 200 ml of hot water, a 10 s flush, all slowed to the same 50 s here), steam's Purging, then Done. The machine's numbers drive each scene: flow and pressure against what is set (how hard it works), the heater (how much steam rises; for hot water, the temperature set), and a colour from cold to warm that the ring takes too: steam's milk warms with its time, hot water's colour is the temperature set, the flush's is the group against its target. Try Machine: Weak and Strong, and Reduced motion, which stills every scene and keeps the colours and the levels, because they are readings. Whatever moves fades out behind the figures, and the fills under them keep the contrast the figures need. Each drink keeps today's scene too, as Simple (not drawn here).

Steam

STEAM · THE DEFAULT · NOZZLE AND SPRAY

Wand and spray

The pitcher from the side: the wand dips in from the right, steam jets from its tip and rolls the milk.
  • Flow and pressure set how hard it sprays and how fast the milk rolls; a wand that sputters, sputters here.
  • The heater against its set sets how much steam rises: a cool heater (Weak) steams thinly.
  • The steam time warms the milk from a cold blue-white to cream, and the ring with it; the milk grows as it takes air, fastest early, as a stretch does, and the tip sinks deeper as it rolls.
  • Hot milk steams: late in the run steam rises off the whole surface, not just the wand. Purging, a puff from the tip; done, the milk settles, glossy.
STEAM · A CHOICE · FROM ABOVE

Whirlpool

Looking down into the pitcher: the milk turns in a whirlpool and foam spirals in from the rim. The tip sits off centre, where a barista holds it.
  • Flow and pressure set how fast it turns; the bubbles start big and get finer as the milk textures.
  • The steam time warms the milk from cold to cream; behind the number it stays a tone the figures read on, and the foam fades out there.
  • Done: the whirlpool coasts to a stop and the surface goes glossy.
STEAM · A CHOICE · LATTE ART

Rosetta

A cup from above: a rosetta pours in, a leaf for every ninth of the steam time, the newest one wiggling in as the pitcher does. Done, the pull-through draws its stem.
  • The steam time grows it and warms the milk; nothing in a cup follows flow or pressure, so they don't show here.
  • The cost: the rosetta grows behind the number. In dark mode the figures carry a halo of crema so they still read; the small "of 50 s" line reads worst.

Hot water

HOT WATER · THE DEFAULT · FROM THE SIDE

Pour

A stream falls from the spout into the cup, and the water rises toward the volume set.
  • Flow sets the stream's width and how fast it runs; the splash and the rings it sends across the surface follow it.
  • The temperature set sets how much the water steams, and its colour: cooler water bluer, hotter warmer, the ring too. The machine reports no live temperature for hot water, so this is the one set.
  • Done: the stream stops, the surface settles, and the water steams on a while.
HOT WATER · A CHOICE · FROM ABOVE

From above

Looking down into the cup: rings spread from where the stream lands, and the water deepens as it fills.
  • Flow sets how often the rings go out; the volume deepens the water; the temperature set, how much vapour drifts over it.
  • Under the number the water stays a tone the figures read on, and the rings fade out there.

Flush

FLUSH · THE DEFAULT · FROM THE SIDE

Shower

The group head's shower screen at the top, water streaming down to the drip tray's grate and splashing there.
  • Flow sets how fast the streams run and how much they splash.
  • The group's temperature against its target colours the water and the ring: blue while the group is cool, warm as it reaches the target, which is what a flush before a shot is for.
FLUSH · A CHOICE · FROM BELOW

Screen

The shower screen seen from below, its holes in rings; drops swell out of them and fall toward you.
  • Flow sets how many drops fall; the group's temperature colours them, as in Shower.
  • Behind the number the screen stays plain, so the figures read.

TABLET · STEAM, THEN GOING BACK BY ITSELF
Steam's default scene in its place, with the readouts and trace moving with the same run.
  • Done, with Go back by itself on: Go back counts down beside its label ("8 s") and drains toward the moment it goes; Stay beside it keeps the view, as does any tap on the view (a stepper, say). Tap Go back to go at once.
  • At zero it returns to the screen the view took over, as Go back does, with location.replace and the tap guard. A screen reader hears it once, as the run ends ("Going back to the coach in 10 seconds"), never every second.
  • It only goes back from a view CafAI switched to by itself, and never while a field has the keyboard, a new drink or a shot starts, or the connection was lost (the view says so).
  • Per drink: each has its own switch and time in Settings > Drink screens. Off, nothing changes from today: the view stays, with Go back.

SETTINGS · DRINK SCREENS
A section of its own, after Steam, hot water and flush: those are Decaid's (held while the machine runs, written to Decaid), these are CafAI's (kept on this device, and live whatever the machine is doing). Laid out as that section is: a group per drink, side by side on a tablet, one row per setting on a phone.
  • Animation: a native select. Steam: Wand and spray, Whirlpool, Rosetta, Simple. Hot water: Pour, From above, Simple. Flush: Shower, Screen, Simple. Simple is today's scene.
  • Go back by itself: a switch, as Sleep when idle's; on, After under it with the drinks' stepper (5 to 60 s, 5 a tap, any whole second typed); off, "The screen stays until you tap Go back." Flush is off here only to show it.
  • On, after 10 s, until someone changes it; each change applies at once, as the temperature unit does.
